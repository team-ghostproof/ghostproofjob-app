Résumé tips

How to get your résumé past the ATS — without keyword stuffing

✍️
By Jett · Sep 26, 2026 · 4 min read

An ATS is just software that parses your résumé into fields and scans for relevant terms. You don't beat it by stuffing keywords — you beat it by being clean, parseable, and honestly matched to the role.

What actually trips the parser

  • Tables, columns, and text boxes. Many parsers read them out of order or drop them entirely — keep the layout single-column.
  • Headers/footers for key info. Contact details buried in the header can vanish; put them in the body.
  • Images and icons for text. A skills graphic is invisible to the scan — write skills as text.
  • Unusual section names. "What I've done" reads worse than "Experience." Use the standard headings.

The keyword fix (the honest way)

  • Pull the 8–10 terms the posting repeats.
  • Mirror the ones you genuinely have — in your real bullets, using their phrasing.
  • Never claim a skill you can't back up in an interview.

A 30-second self-check
