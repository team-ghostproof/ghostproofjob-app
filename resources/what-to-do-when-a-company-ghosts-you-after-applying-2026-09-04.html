What to do when a company ghosts you after applying

By The GhostProofJob Team · Sep 4, 2026 · 4 min read

Being ghosted after an application — or worse, an interview — is common and demoralizing. It is not a reflection of your worth. Here's what actually helps.

A practical playbook

  • Send one polite follow-up. After ~7–10 business days, a short note restating your interest is reasonable — once, not five times.
  • Set a mental deadline. No reply within two weeks of that follow-up? Treat it as a no and move your energy forward.
  • Keep applying in parallel. Never let one role become your only iron in the fire — volume protects your morale.
  • Log it. Record which companies went quiet, so the pattern becomes visible instead of just painful.

Turn a wasted hour into a warning

On GhostProofJob, filing a ghost report on a company that went silent adds to its community ghosting-risk score — so the next applicant sees the pattern before they invest. Your bad experience becomes someone else's saved week.

Protect your energy

Ghosting is about the employer's broken process, not your candidacy. Favor verified employers and roles that post pay — they tend to run tighter, more respectful hiring.
