Ghost jobs

5 signs a job posting is a ghost

✍️
By Jett · Aug 28, 2026 · 4 min read

A ghost job looks real, wastes your time, and never closes. After flagging thousands of them, here are the patterns worth watching.

The signals

  • It never closes. A role that’s been "open" for months with no updates is often a pipeline-filler.
  • Vague everything. No real responsibilities, no team, no manager named — just buzzwords.
  • Reposted on a loop. The same listing reappears every few weeks with a fresh date.
  • No salary, ever. Serious, funded roles increasingly post a range; perpetual silence is a flag.
  • Radio silence after applying. Weeks of nothing, no confirmation, no rejection.

What to do instead

Favor verified employers, apply early, and log the ones that go quiet. On GhostProofJob, a ghost report warns the next applicant — turning your wasted hour into someone else's saved one.
