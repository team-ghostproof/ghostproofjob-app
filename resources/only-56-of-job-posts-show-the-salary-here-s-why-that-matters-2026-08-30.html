Only 56% of job posts show the salary. Here's why that matters

By The GhostProofJob Team · Aug 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Pay transparency is spreading, but slowly. We checked how many of the 225,625 verified roles in our database actually post a salary.

56%
of the 225,625 open verified roles disclose a salary or salary range up front.

That means most postings still make you invest an application — sometimes an interview — before you learn whether the pay is even in range. Roles that post pay tend to be more serious and further along in their process.

How to use it

When two roles look similar, favor the one that posts pay: it respects your time and signals a real, funded opening rather than a pipeline-filler. And always research a range before you talk numbers.

Source: GhostProofJob verified-jobs database, as of Aug 30, 2026. A role counts as transparent if it posts a salary figure or range. Counts reflect active roles still accepting applicants and update automatically. Roles are grouped by title keyword, so treat fields as directional, not exact.
