Which fields are hiring remote right now

By The GhostProofJob Team · Aug 19, 2026 · 4 min read

Everyone argues about whether remote work is shrinking. Instead of guessing, we counted every verified remote role in our database and grouped it by field.

7,062
verified remote Sales roles are open right now — the clear #1, ahead of Marketing (4,414).

The full ranking

Sales leads, but the more useful story is further down: fields like Marketing and Healthcare post steadily and draw a fraction of the applicant crush that the top spot does. If you're flexible on field, that gap is where the interviews hide.

What to do with this

Apply where the ratio favors you, not only where the volume is loudest. Remote roles move fast — a tailored résumé submitted early beats a generic one submitted late.

Source: GhostProofJob verified-jobs database, as of Aug 19, 2026. Remote roles are those flagged remote by the employer or aggregator. Counts reflect active roles still accepting applicants and update automatically. Roles are grouped by title keyword, so treat fields as directional, not exact.
